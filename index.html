One search instead of twenty tabs.

Plats covers more than 120 of Stockholm's best restaurants, and you can add any other place you like.

01

Every booking page at once

Plats reads each restaurant's own booking page, including bar and counter seats, and shows only the times that fit your evening.

02

Told when a table opens

Fully booked? Set an alert for the date and time you want, and Plats lets you know when a cancellation frees a table.

03

Lists that fit the occasion

Plats comes with two hand-picked lists to get you started. Build your own for the business lunch in the city or a Friday dinner on Söder: filter by area, lunch or dinner and price, and Plats suggests places that fit.

04

Every booking in one place

Your next table sits on the home screen with the map, the menu and the calendar a tap away. Share it with the people you're dining with.

You book with the restaurant.

  • The booking happens on the restaurant's own page, inside the app, with your details filled in.
  • Card details, when a restaurant asks for them, go straight to its booking page. Plats never handles payments.
  • No account. Your lists and bookings stay on your phone.
